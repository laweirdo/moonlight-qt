# Bulan — creative philosophy

**Moonlight is a tool. Bulan is a place you arrive.** Bulan should feel like
first-party console system software made with care: confident, warm and fully at
home beside a console interface.

## Reflection

The moon reflects light; Bulan reflects the user's PC. This is the product
metaphor, not decoration: warm source above, cool response below; a chosen game
and the machine carrying it; motion that hands one state into the next.

## Balance

- Layout, icons and chrome are strictly minimal.
- Focus, motion, sound and rounding carry the playfulness.
- The interface is composed when still and delighted when touched.
- Whimsy is a precise response, not constant movement or ornament.

## Voice

Warm, brief, second-person and quietly confident. Technical language belongs in
advanced settings and logs, never on a front-facing recovery screen.

## Guardrails

- Steam Deck first: controller-only operation, visible focus and recoverable
  navigation at 1280×800.
- Use console discipline—contrast, speed, spacing and restraint—without copying
  another platform's assets or chrome.
- Custom Bulan visual components only.
- Preserve Moonlight's discovery, pairing, streaming and platform foundation.
- Bulan is the user-facing project; Moonlight remains clearly credited as its
  GPLv3 upstream.
